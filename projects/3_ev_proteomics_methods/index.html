EV isolation and proteomics methods

Reproducible extracellular vesicle isolation and DIA mass spectrometry workflows

Biomarker results are only as reliable as the methods behind them. EVs are a small fraction of what is in plasma, so how they are isolated, stored and measured has a large effect on what is found.

This work develops and benchmarks the workflows that the other projects depend on:

  • Isolation. High-throughput immunoaffinity isolation of EVs that is compatible with clinical laboratory automation (Khanabdali et al., 2024), and storage conditions that preserve EVs (Walker et al., 2022). I contributed to the community MISEV2023 reporting guidelines (Welsh et al., 2024).
  • Mass spectrometry. Data-independent acquisition (DIA) proteomics on Orbitrap Astral and ZenoTOF 7600 platforms, from early SWATH-MS studies (Jayabalan et al., 2019) to deep profiling of blood components and their EVs (Hwang et al., 2024), alongside targeted assays for placental EVs (Lai et al., 2022).
  • Data analysis. Pipelines that take raw DIA data through to candidate biomarkers, run on high-performance computing for large multi-site cohorts.
