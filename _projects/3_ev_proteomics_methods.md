Biomarker results are only as reliable as the methods behind them. EVs are a small fraction of what is in plasma, so how they are isolated, stored and measured has a large effect on what is found.

This work develops and benchmarks the workflows that the other projects depend on:

- **Isolation.** High-throughput immunoaffinity isolation of EVs that is compatible with clinical laboratory automation {% cite RN71 %}, and storage conditions that preserve EVs {% cite RN61 %}. I contributed to the community MISEV2023 reporting guidelines {% cite RN67 %}.
- **Mass spectrometry.** Data-independent acquisition (DIA) proteomics on Orbitrap Astral and ZenoTOF 7600 platforms, from early SWATH-MS studies {% cite RN28 %} to deep profiling of blood components and their EVs {% cite RN73 %}, alongside targeted assays for placental EVs {% cite RN58 %}.
- **Data analysis.** Pipelines that take raw DIA data through to candidate biomarkers, run on high-performance computing for large multi-site cohorts.
