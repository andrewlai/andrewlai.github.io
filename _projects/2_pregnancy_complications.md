The placenta releases extracellular vesicles (EVs) into the mother's blood throughout pregnancy. Their number and cargo change with placental health, so they offer a window into the placenta that a standard blood test cannot.

Our work in this area covers:

- **Screening.** A rapid, high-sensitivity assay that profiles circulating placental EVs to screen for pregnancy complications {% cite palma2025rapid %}.
- **Gestational diabetes.** How EVs from adipose tissue and the placenta regulate glucose metabolism and fetal growth {% cite jayabalan2025extracellular RN27 RN44 %}.
- **Preeclampsia.** Hypoxia-induced EV proteins that regulate inflammation and blood pressure {% cite RN43 %}.
- **Preterm birth.** Longitudinal changes in circulating EV proteins and miRNAs across term and preterm pregnancies {% cite RN26 RN41 %}.
